# Codex Batavorum — wiki hub

**Status:** Front door. **Pointer hub.** This page does **not** add canon. Law lives in the linked files.  
**Who:** **Batavian Iron-Guard** (*Cohors Batavorum*). Civic name *Der Batav*. Motto **Unbent**. Battle answer **We hold.**  
**Lean:** They prefer to **hold the wall** — attrition and siege-density under **Friction Geometry** — not lightning assault, terror-cult, or “better at everything.”  
**In a nutshell:** Space Marines with social anxiety and an identity crisis. They adapt; other Imperials **read them wrong**.

---

## Open Imperial filing (do not “clean up”)

* **Founding / progenitor:** **unknown** or **redacted** on tithe scrolls. Wolf-marks are **phenotype**. Gene formula does not write the Chapter. [`progenitor-classification.md`](personae-command-index/doctrine-and-organs/progenitor-classification.md)
* **Job:** wall against the tide at **Insula Tenebrarum** / **Castra Vetera** (Nine Phalanx, Ultima Segmentum, Imperium Nihilus). Chapter world **Noviomagus**. [`castra-vetera-galactic-lock.md`](atlas-and-topography/castra-vetera-galactic-lock.md)
* **Size:** **5,000** / ten **Vexilla** — *Codex* breach as friction conveyor, not vanity. [`pax-batavorum.md`](personae-command-index/doctrine-and-organs/pax-batavorum.md)
* **Faith:** they honor the divinity the Emperor **carries** — oath, Throne-weight, **Ancestor-Sovereign** — not Ministorum god-worship.
* **Birth-war:** **the Reclamation** (pre-Cadia). Forbidden name: Foundation War. [`the-reclamation.md`](personae-command-index/events-and-chronologies/the-reclamation.md)
