# Codex Batavorum — revision of the *Codex Astartes*

**Status:** Doctrine lock. **Not** a dump of Guilliman’s book. **Not** a symptom of Wolf’s Curse.  
**When:** Written in the field across **the Reclamation** and **the Hold** (pre-Cadia), then kept. Applications still valuable **now**.  
**Origin war:** [`the-reclamation.md`](../events-and-chronologies/the-reclamation.md)  
**Curse (separate track):** [`wolfs-curse-origin-and-den.md`](wolfs-curse-origin-and-den.md)

---

## I. Spine and revision

The *Codex Astartes* remains the **spine**. The Chapter does not erase it because the islands were ugly or the book had no page for a given xenos.

What changes is **application** and the strict **“in this situation, do this”** tables — slowly, at the speed of the Reclamation, under **Alaric** and the officers who live the geometry.

**Batavi revision** = living field law: friction geometry, Shared Sweat, *Axiom of Reason*, Vexilla mesh, super-tithe, *Instrumentum Solum*. It is **rational**. Curse scenes may **interfere**. They do not **replace** this work.

---

## II. What not to write

* Do not hitch Furor to “the *Codex* failed, so they snapped.”  
* Do not stage a single council where the book is burned.  
* Do not treat later Primaris method as the moment tactics become Batavi — the revision is **older** than the Rubicon.

---

## III. Open vs sealed

Open Imperial: divergent Chapter, non-compliant strength, still recognizably *Codex*-descended in squad grain.  
Sealed: the revision is the **price of holding an island the book was not written to hold** — still a book, still a spine.
