## I. Design law

Each **classic *Codex* mastery** or **invented Legatus mastery** must carry the **same narrative weight** as a standard Chapter captain honorific — **Chapter-wide authority** over one class of problem, not mesh clerk work or duplicate organ heads.

| Rule | Lock |
| ---- | ---- |
| **One scroll title per Legatus** | Each **Legatus PRIMA–NONA** holds **exactly one** Chapter mastery (classic **or** invented). Seat theater stays in [`legati-operational-register.md`](../../arsenal-and-logistics/legati-operational-register.md). **Four** also hold a **cohort-chief lattice hat** — not a second mastery ([`military-hierarchy.md`](military-hierarchy.md) §3.5). |
| **Sole holder** | One incumbent per classic lane; no dual Legati on Watch, Fleet, etc. |
| **DECIMA supremes** | **Alaric**, **Drusus**, **Varro**, **Kadmos**, **Otho** hold organ posts; **Graile** holds **Lord Executioner** / Emperor’s Wolf; **Custos Ascensum** holds ascent (classic X translation). |
| **Not on Legati** | **Master of the Marches** (Castellan function) · **Chief Victualler** (no Astartes counterpart) · **Master of the Culture** (distributed + *Librarium* archive) |

**Weight test (before any new title):** *What does the whole Chapter owe this office when the problem strikes any Vexilla?*

## IV. Invented masteries — scope (Codex weight)

### Master of the Law — SECVNDA

**Lawforce, not judge.** Chapter-wide **Imperial Lex** kill geometry: measured fire under witness-bearing structures, **Silence Cohort** executive coordination with **Adeptus Arbites**, assigned-target packets, capture chains to public law. **Not** *Reclusiam* penance (**Judex**) · **not** general substrata intake (*Librarium* / veteran commanders). [`lex-imperialis-iron-justice.md`](../../political-relations/lex-imperialis-iron-justice.md)

### Master of the Breach — TERTIA

**All violent entry** — thermal, chem, oxygen-stack **first penetration**; when the Chapter must **open** a hole before it can hold or siege (**hours**, not a campaign). Distinct from **Siege** (grind) and **Anchor** (hold).

### Master of the Anchor — QVARTA

**All forbidden retreats** — static holds under tyranid veil, high-G trench, **Shadow in the Warp** pressure; **hold geometry** once the Castellan places you. **Not** hunt or pursuit.

### Master of the Hunt — QVINTA

**All pursuit of enemies who wronged the mesh and escaped** — Orks, Drukhari, raiders, **inside or outside** the mesh; authority to **commit hunt Vexilla** until quarry is dead, sealed, or judged exhausted (Armageddon-class pursuit, not one battle). **Not** apex duel (**Lord Executioner**) · **not** Silence pattern work in hives (**Law**).

### Master of the Siege — SEXTA

**All fortress reduction** — when walls must come down over **days**: trains, **Indomitor**, twin breach, vault war; when siege assets commit Chapter-wide. Seat offices ***Maior Caementarius*** / ***Pater Murorum*** remain **fortress-design** audits to Castellan; **mastery scroll** = **war** office. Distinct from **Breach** (*we need a door*).

### Master of the Vent — SEPTIMA

**All plasma / photonic energy kill** — vent windows, **Null-Vent** law, when the Chapter fields **energy weapons** at scale. **Not** general munitions (**Arsenal**).
