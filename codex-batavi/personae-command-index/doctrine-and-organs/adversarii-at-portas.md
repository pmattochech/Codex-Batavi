## 1. What the name is

***Adversarii at Portas*** is the Chapter’s **last measure**: the hour when every other protocol and line has already failed, **PRIMA–NONA** did not hold, and survivors have reunited on **Noviomagus** under the **DECIMA** banner. Everything is then at cost. It is a desperate move, not a Vexilla crisis and not a SEXTA siege.

It is **not exclusively** a Versibar protocol. Black Cell release is **one instrument** inside this hour. So is the war-wake of **Radulf Castrin** (*The Old Wolf*) — the **only** Dreadnought still in reserve when Portas is declared (every other coffin was already spent on earlier warrants).

**Full Portas**, as defined here, is **unfired law** on the present register. Do not back-date a remnant-on-Noviomagus declaration onto Malakor or onto later local cage spends.

---

## 2. What the name is not

**Local Black Cell open** is a **separate** seat weapon. A fortress can die on a frontier world and the cells there can still open **without** declaring Portas, **without** waking Radulf, and **without** a Castellan seal.

Do **not** file every Versibar area-denial as *Adversarii at Portas*.

---

## 3. Local cell-open (not Portas)

*Medicinae* **owns the lock**: who sits in which cell, witness vs feral, clinical bridle ([`dossier-drusus.md`](../character-dossiers/triumvirate/dossier-drusus.md)). The **open** is war plus soul. Drusus may file the bill after a frontier open; he does **not** co-sign **PRIMA–NONA** local release.

| Seat | Keys | Notes |
|------|------|--------|
| **PRIMA–NONA** | **Legatus** + **chief Wolfkeeper of that Vexilla** | Two keys. Not a captain. Not a Judiciar. **Not** automatically the **Confessor Vexilli** hat (penance and marks, not death). If the Confessor is also the chief Wolfkeeper, that is one man two hats, not a law that penance-office owns the cage. |
| **DECIMA** | the **Triumvirate** (Alaric, Drusus, Varro) as a **court** | No Legatus, no *Curia Vexilli*. Capital cages are harder: clinic **does** sit on this key because Drusus is one of the three. Not Alaric borrowing Varro’s hat. Not the Emperor’s Wolf. |

**Feral allocation:** a Versibar with no measurable cognitive residue is reclassified for **local cell-open spend** or **execution** on clinical assessment — not “Portas allocation” as a default stamp. Beast without memory teaches neophytes nothing.

## 4. Instruments when Portas is declared

When the remnant stands on Noviomagus under DECIMA:

1. **Black Cells** still shut may open — Versibars as area denial. Terminal form is **indiscriminate by design** (closed-space predation math). Local opens may already have happened **on the way down**.
2. **Radulf** war-wake — **Alaric only**. The Emperor’s Wolf does **not** spend him. Graile still lunges and still bears the **justice vexillum**. The Old Wolf is Castellan-reserved DECIMA iron, parallel to *Castra-Lupus*.
3. Further last-kit (fleet suicide geometry, tithe-burn, etc.) belongs on this seal or it does not belong.

**Radulf** is **DECIMA**, counted inside the Tenth’s **500**. Combat deploy on this protocol **only**. Detail: [`dreadnought-sarcophagus-law.md`](dreadnought-sarcophagus-law.md) §4.

## 5. Malakor — correlation, not declaration

**Valerian Heresy / Malakor V (050.M42)** remains the first **documented battlefield correlation**: *the Cull* Tier III object-fixation on traitor gene-seed plus Lord Castellan **Alpha** predation corridor — aimed loyal Versibars, not Chapter-wide catastrophe, **not** nine seats failed, **not** remnant-on-Noviomagus, **not** Radulf walking.

*Medicinae* files Malakor as **correlation** to Portas *instruments* (vectored Versibar mass) **without** a full Portas declaration. It does **not** revoke classification that **unaimed** Versibar remains strategic hazard. Terminal Portas remains indiscriminate **by design**.

Chronicle and AAR language that once collapsed “cell-open” into this Latin name is **legacy phrasing**; living law is this page.
