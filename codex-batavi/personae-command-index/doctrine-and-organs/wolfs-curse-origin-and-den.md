## I. What this is not

* **Not** a Chaos god, daemon, or possession path. Execratio remains **Warp-uncorrupted** catastrophic failure of a claimed body ([encyclopedia §B](../../biological-encyclopedia-bestiary/projection-aurea-wolfs-curse.md)).
* **Not** proof of Fenris, the Canis Helix, or any parent Legion. Wolf marks stay **phenotype and curse**, not a progenitor stamp.
* **Not** a stamp that lives only in the gene-seed vial. High Lords assays can pass. The curse is **den plus people**.
* **Not** “the *Codex* failed, so they snapped.” Tactical revision of the *Codex Astartes* is **Alaric’s** long field work. Curse scenes may interfere with that work. They are not that work.
* **Not** a fourth ancestor. Batavi voice still counts **only the Emperor**. This presence is **not worshipped**.

Harnessed disciplines (Wild Hunt, March of the Machine, the Cull) remain the **aimed** use of chimeric fuel. Wolf’s Curse remains the same system going **autoimmune** — protection that does not love you and will not always stop at the right body.

## II. Origin (sealed working lock)

**Terra is the species of the spirit. Castra Vetera is the den.**

An **Old Terran** wolf-presence — leftover of Old Earth’s wild, not a named Ministorum saint, not a Fenrisian god — took the closed ecology of **Insula Tenebrarum** as den. Isolation gave it a job: keep the forgotten-tithe remnant **mostly** clean of the large corruptions that should have eaten an unreachable mesh. “Mostly” is the grain. Some still fall. The vortex and the tide remain real.

When the Chapter became Batavi on this island, the loudest living bodies became its hunters. The chimeric stack is the **socket**. The presence is the **tenant**. Drusus may still file a genetic ratchet once the claim is in; Varro may treat a landlord. Both can be looking at the same animal from different rooms.

**Writer crib (out of universe, not in-world text):** Innistrad werewolves as a plane’s immune system that also eats the village. Use the mapping; do not name that setting in `codex-batavi/` prose.

## III. People, blood, and the vial

| Condition | Curse |
|-----------|--------|
| Gene-seed sample on Terra / in a freezer | **Clean of the tenant.** Chimeric predisposition may still read as architecture. Purity paperwork can pass. |
| Marine grown from Batavi seed **outside** the mesh, never of the people | **No curse** while he remains outside. He is not Batavi. |
| That same marine **enters Castra Vetera / Insula Tenebrarum** without Batavi raising | The den recognizes the **socket** and claims him. Without *Axiom*, pack, and raising there is no hunter — only a host. The ladder is the same; the brakes are absent. Furor → Exanimus → Execratio may dump in a **short window**. Treat as **unraised exception**, not a rewrite of career law for brothers who belong. |
| Batavi (of the people, raised here or adopted here) **outside** the mesh | **Still carries it.** Geography is optional after the claim. Stages are not healed by leaving. |
| Local aspirant / Rubicon brother, raised as Batavi | **Normal clinical law.** Crossing or implantation in the den is infection; **raising** is the difference between a brother and a Versibar on the dock. |

**Frontier (this pass):** the claim line is **Castra Vetera weather** — the polity / mesh of **Insula Tenebrarum**, not “any warp jump.” This file does **not** split hull-in-void versus boots-on-Noviomagus. Both are inside the den until atlas says otherwise.

**Frequency:** the unraised cascade is **metaphysically true**. In-story it may be **once**, or never witnessed, or Varro’s private bet. Do not make dock-Versibars a routine import screen.

Open Administratum will file **local warp pollution** or **unreplicable gene-flaw** and move on. They cannot put a finished marine under this sky, wait for the body-and-mind chord, and still call it a vial test.

## V. Mortals of the den

The presence kept **the largest part** of Castra’s human remnant free of the big corruptions during isolation. It is **not** a perfect filter and **not** a mortal werewolf plague on the open record. Full hunter-office sits in **Astartes** sockets. Thinner mortal bite is **not** locked this pass.
