## I. Gift of method, not sons

After the **Valerian Heresy** bloodletting, the Throne / *Indomitus*-era logistics send **technology**: how to fabricate **Primaris** chassis and how Firstborn who already belong may **cross the Rubicon**.

**Forbidden this lock:** Grey Shield / Torchbearer **battle-brother tranches** as Chapter replenishment. No imported sons through **Witness Ford**. Terra cannot staff this den with outsiders.

The insult (Alaric’s silent filing) can survive: they sent a **manual** when the Wall had already been paid in Firstborn — too late to feel like mercy, and not a box of brothers.

**Cawl** as architect of the reactive template remains **sealed Triumvirate conviction**, not open-record chatter ([`progenitor-classification.md`](progenitor-classification.md)).

## II. Sequence

1. **Eligible Firstborn convert.** Brothers who already have den, blood, raising, and the curse they carry. Rubicon is a **trauma spike** on the existing ladder — often a first **Furor**, sometimes a bad **Exanimus** window — not a three-stage execution on the dock.
2. **Thereafter, all new marines are Primaris**, made on **Noviomagus**, raised as Batavi from implantation. Custos circuit, *Axiom*, pack: they are **of the people**. They pick up the curse under **normal** clinical law, on a hotter stack.
3. **Crust, not a second Chapter.** Converted veterans remain. Firstborn who **cannot** cross stay Firstborn until death.

**~5,000 ceiling:** local raising and conversion make illegal scale **executable**. The collar does **not** require imported bodies. See chronology §III.

## IV. What later prose must not do

* Do not dock a wave of unraised Primaris as mercy. That is how you get Versibars, not a Chapter.
* Do not treat Rubicon as a cure. The curse is not in the old chassis alone.
* Do not use “Primaris mass reinforcement” as the cause of the **5,000** decision in **new** staff text. Templates and forges, yes. Foreign battle-brothers, no.
* Pre-method line remains **Firstborn / Firstboot** in chronology even where older observer prose says “Primaris-height” as a bulk gloss (Castellan pre-abyss scale stays a separate chassis fact in his dossier).
