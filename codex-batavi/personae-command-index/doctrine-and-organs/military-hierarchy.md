# THE FRICTION CHAIN (CHAPTER MILITARY HIERARCHY)

The *Cohors Batavorum* war machine is a mathematical aberration sustained by survival thermodynamics. To run a 5,000-warrior contingent on the lethal **Insula Tenebrarum** / **Witness Ford** frontier, the Chapter ignored *Codex Astartes* bureaucracy and adopted a command chain built purely to kill logistical bottlenecks. The hierarchy below governs how our **Friction Geometry** applies from trench melee to macro-orbital obliteration.

## 1. RAW STOCK AND STANDARD TOOL (BASE OF EACH VEXILLA)

**The neophyte (ore in the forge):** The recruit is not yet a gear; he is a block of mortal flesh being brutally machined. The neophyte endures continuous lethality stages on Aethelgard and Incus-Gravis, surviving extreme atmospheric and gravitational pressure. His biology seethes endocrinally, fighting to tame gene-seed’s first shock. They operate as scouts and heavy labor, burning aggression through physical effort so Wolf’s Curse terror shatters their minds before they receive ceramite plates.

**The battle-brother (the Batavi):** Line infantry and the mathematical base of our war. A single Batavi is the perfected transhuman tool: ego-stripped, vanity-immune, focused on calculated kills. Under the *Axioma Rationis*, his mind works as a cold dam against his own biology’s horrors. In *Zone Mortalis* they use the *second blade* (armored body as blunt weapon) and march in *Analytical Silence* — no battle cries or futile fanaticism. The mass of these warriors, the Batavi, forms the impassable Iron Wall.

## 2. TACTICAL EXECUTORS (KILL MATH)

**The sergeant (squad calculator):** Direct tactical lead for a **squad** (5–10 Batavi). The sergeant does not inspire with glorious shouts; he sets fire rhythm, cover angles, and ammo efficiency for his cell. They are the front line’s pressure regulators. When chimeric predisposition fuel rises toward **Wild Hunt** or **March of the Machine** depth, the sergeant holds formation to **authorized** harness bands and enforces rotation cues from the ***Curia Vexilli*** relay — not uncontrolled metabolic burn.

**The lieutenant (*Zone Mortalis* executor):** Maestro of short-range slaughter. Commanding a **demi-company** (strict fifty-warrior fraction — two per **company**), the lieutenant is the officer who descends into the abattoir. While upper chain draws the siege, the lieutenant applies **Friction Geometry** millimeter by millimeter in choking corridors and mud trenches. He translates macro-strategy into direct execution and advance orders, ensuring enemy blood spills in optimized patterns. **Demi-company** is the *Codex* size label; the lieutenant holds **no** Chapter mastery scroll and **no** unique named specialty team.

## 3. MACRO-COMMAND AND WELL GOVERNORS (VEXILLA STRUCTURE)

**The captain (macro-strategist):** Commander of a **company** (100 battle-brothers — mixed packet). Directly subordinate to the Legatus, the captain runs planetary operations theater. He orchestrates bombardment vectors, adamantium supply routes, and kill-zone overlap. His job is to ensure before the **Vexilla** physically hits the threat, the enemy is already logistically mauled by trench positioning. A company is **two demi-companies** glued for war — **never** a standing block of fifty specialist cohort brothers of one type as the whole command. The captain does **not** bear the Vexilla banner.

**The Ancient (standard bearer):** One per **Vexilla** (**PRIMA–NONA**). Vanilla English office — Codex **Ancient**, not a Batavi coinage. He carries the **Vexilla banner** (default Chapter face + **LATIN-CAPS** ordinal; **PRIMA** adds the **obligation** mark). He stands in the **Legatus retinue**, not in a captain’s command squad. He must **not drop the cloth while he lives**. Counted inside the **500** (typically a pure-line veteran attached to the retinue) — **not** a 501st body and **not** five company Ancients. **DECIMA** has **no** Vexilla Ancient; the **justice vexillum** stays on the **Emperor’s Wolf**. **Radulf LVI-1** (*The Old Wolf*) remains **Contemptor Dreadnought ancient** — same English word, **different office**. [vexilla-by-vexilla-design.md](../intro-and-heraldry/vexilla-by-vexilla-design.md).

**The Legatus / Elder-Master (system pillar):** Decentralized military governor and supreme authority over one of the Nine Phalanx’s nine worlds / super-systems (3×3 geographic block — do not confuse with the tactical **Vexilla**). A Legatus commands one **Vexilla** (indivisible force of 500 Batavi). Filed as ***vir in acie*** — **the man in the line** — sovereign among his five hundred, identity in the ***Alabarda*** and command geometry, **not** in Castellan-class artificer armor ([`castra-lupus-doctrine.md`](../../arsenal-and-logistics/castra-lupus-doctrine.md) §1.1; per-Legatus register: [`legati-operational-register.md`](../../arsenal-and-logistics/legati-operational-register.md)). He bears the ***Alabarda***: a **halberd** (combat polearm — planted blade, strike head, hook geometry per *Armourium* pattern). **No cloth on the haft.** The unit’s **Vexilla banner** (default Chapter face + **LATIN-CAPS** ordinal — the **same heraldic charges** as the formal seal; **PRIMA** adds **obligation**) is borne by the **Ancient** in the Legatus retinue ([`intro-and-heraldry.md`](../intro-and-heraldry/chapter-identity.md); [vexilla-by-vexilla-design.md](../intro-and-heraldry/vexilla-by-vexilla-design.md)). Brothers name the seat in **LATIN-CAPS** only (**PRIMA**…**NONA**; **DECIMA** for the Castellan anchor) — unit and cloth still share the word **Vexilla**. The *Alabarda* haft integrates a **command relay** that pushes merged tactical *auspex*, **Genetor Vexilli** clearance flags, and biological-failure telemetry to orange-lensed troop visors. *Strategium* files **two** orientation instruments under *Analytical Silence* and broken vox: **Legatus pole** (where he is) and **Ancient cloth** (what the five hundred are). **PRIMA** obligation mark: **debt the lattice carries**, not inherited personal guilt — [vexilla-by-vexilla-design.md](../intro-and-heraldry/vexilla-by-vexilla-design.md) §2.2. The Legatus is the cold, autonomous extension of Triumvirate will. Where he plants the blade, retreat is mathematically nullified.

***Alabarda* head and haft:** The **head group** (and certified **haft modes** where issued) varies by **Vexilla** theater — *Armourium* **Types** **Primus** through **Fulmen**: [alabarda-head-groups.md](../../arsenal-and-logistics/alabarda-head-groups.md). **Runtime** actuation — Alabarda plant, length mode, head use in contact — is **sole Legatus authority**; **no** subordinate or *Strategium* **approval** is doctrine for those choices. *Armourium* certifies **equipment safety** at manufacture/refit only. **Cloth** is **Ancient** authority (furl, plant, carry).

**Deathwatch prerequisite (Chapter law):** Promulgated **after the fall of the First Legatus** — **Valerius** eliminated at **Malakor V (050.M42)** and struck from the rolls; the prerequisite was **not** in force during his tenure. *Strategium* codified the rule ***ca.* 051.M42** as part of the post-traitor command lattice. No Marine may be **nominated or confirmed** as **Legatus** unless *Medicinae* and *Strategium* certify **at least one full certified *Watch cycle*** (Deathwatch deployment from Chapter embarkation through **return audit** — definition and liaison seals: [deathwatch-and-batavorum.md](../../political-relations/deathwatch-and-batavorum.md)). **Pre-decree incumbency (*clausula retentionis antecedentis*):** Legati who already held the *Alabarda* when the **051.M42** seal took effect **retain** command **without** retroactive Watch service — *Strategium* refuses to **vacate** lawful governors to satisfy a **new** proof. **Replacement** appointments must satisfy the prerequisite. **Triumvirate unanimous waiver** may void the requirement for a named successor only in **documented extremis** (*Vermilion* entry required).

**The DECIMA and the Lord Castellan (anchor guard):** The absolute apex of our machine. Unlike PRIMA–NONA garrisoning frontier systems under their Legati, the **DECIMA** operates **without intermediate Legatus**: lethal personal escort to the Lord Castellan, symbiotic with the incumbent’s ***Castra-Lupus*** artificer armor (current configuration: ***Castra-Lupus — Helis***, Alaric von Helis). **Tithe window:** DECIMA is the **core escort** of the **~20-year** super-tithe convoy — twenty years of spoil in wrong hands is another Badab; the Castellan **holds** the mesh while DECIMA and the **Emperor’s Wolf** ride the hulls. Other Vexilla may **attach** under DECIMA for that run only. Fence: [insula-tenebrarum-isolation-fence.md](../../atlas-and-topography/insula-tenebrarum-isolation-fence.md) §IV. ***Castra-Lupus*** is the **office name** for the Castellan’s one-of-one armor commission — parallel to a Legatus’s unique ***Alabarda* head group**, not a relic suit passed between commanders; ***Caput Ferreum Castellani*** (*Iron Head*) is **regalia** inherited with the title. Full law: [castra-lupus-doctrine.md](../../arsenal-and-logistics/castra-lupus-doctrine.md). Its members are elite in psychological stability and friction efficiency; **veteran instructors** from the Tenth deploy across the other nine Vexilla to train **specialty cohort** contingents **once those stripes exist** (Hold scars **after Valerian** — [specialty-cohorts.md](../../arsenal-and-logistics/specialty-cohorts.md) §§3–3.3; see also [vexilla-and-visual-summary.md](../../arsenal-and-logistics/vexilla-and-visual-summary.md)). **Battle standard:** DECIMA flies the **justice vexillum** — **feral wolf slain by sword** (register of **Malakor V** and First Legatus treason ended) — not a tenth *Alabarda* duplicate; detail [vexilla-by-vexilla-design.md](../intro-and-heraldry/vexilla-by-vexilla-design.md) §4. **Primary bearer in field:** the **LVPVS IMPERATORIS** (see below).

**LVPVS IMPERATORIS (Emperor’s Wolf):** The Chapter’s **Champion office** — one incumbent at a time, filed in **heraldic inscription Latin** as **LVPVS IMPERATORIS** (*wolf of the Emperor*); English scrolls and barracks: **Emperor’s Wolf**. Imperial Administratum gloss: **Emperor’s Champion** / **Chapter Champion**. **Classic scroll:** **Lord Executioner** (VIII) — high-value kill in the moment; distinct from **Master of the Hunt** (campaign pursuit). **Not** a Legatus: no semi-independent **Vexilla** seat, no **Legatus Ring** vote (witness only when seated), no ***Alabarda***. Does **not** bear a Vexilla banner (that is **Ancient**, PRIMA–NONA). **Legatus-weight in honor** — neophytes, line brothers, veterans, and Legati read the office as **Chapter honor** and **absolute loyalty** made visible: **THE BATAV** (myth predator in oath-service) and **THE ASTARTES** (*Instrumentum Solum* perfected) at once.

**Command chain (DECIMA battle grid):** **Lord Castellan** → **LVPVS IMPERATORIS** → captains / lieutenants / sergeants of **DECIMA**. The Castellan **holds** the wall; the Emperor’s Wolf **lunges** when something must die — enemy HQ, highest threat, **or the 20-year tithe convoy** — so Alaric need not leave hold geometry. The five **supreme posts** embedded in **DECIMA** (Lord Castellan, Chief Apothecary, Master Chaplain, Master of the Forge, Chief Librarian) retain **Triumvirate / organ** authority in their lanes; they are **not** subordinate to the Emperor’s Wolf for *Medicinae*, *Reclusiam*, *Armourium*, or *Librarium* law.

**Relic pair — *Par Ferrum Lupi*:** **Decreto** (artificer **power sword** — the **man**, decree **issued**) and **Termini** (artificer **gladius** — the **wolf**, termination **delivered**). Filed sequence: decree first, termination second. Close combat doctrine: ***Modus Binorum Ferrum*** (*two-iron mode* — alternating long/short lines; agile paired flow under *Analytical Silence*). Chronicle prose may nickname **Termini** as **the Bite**. Full register: [weapons-and-equipment-catalog.md](../../arsenal-and-logistics/weapons-and-equipment-catalog.md). **Incumbent:** **Markus Graile** — [dossier-markus-graile.md](../character-dossiers/cohorts-and-detachments/dossier-markus-graile.md).

**Faith filing:** Open record — Throne **instrument** under *Faith Protocol* (*The Emperor dictates, we comply.*), not Ministorum **God-Emperor** theater. Inner *Reclusiam* gloss may read **Ancestor-Sovereign’s wolf** without changing the stamped title.

**DECIMA entry requirements (summary):** veteran status or isolated heroism is insufficient. The candidate must **(1)** complete **at least one full certified *Watch cycle*** (same definition as Legatus prerequisite — [deathwatch-and-batavorum.md](../../political-relations/deathwatch-and-batavorum.md)); **(2)** complete **proven minimum time in line infantry** across Suppression, Ruin, and Siege divisions — “tool” before “elite function”; **(3)** serve **in each of the four specialty cohorts**, in organic fifty-warrior detachments per Vexilla, for **at least one campaign cycle** (or *Strategium*-equivalent interval) **per cohort**, with aptitude files signed by home Legatus and Tenth instructor; **(4)** rank **at the top of his pool** (logistical precision, documented kill ratio, zero *Axiom of Reason* discipline failures in audit windows); **(5)** endure the **Batav Wolf trial** — protocol confrontation with a *Canis Batavorum* specimen in a containment chamber, imposing **absolute dominance** on the beast **without** unnecessary death **and without** losing **Furor** containment (species + trial law: [batav-wolf-canis-batavorum.md](../../biological-encyclopedia-bestiary/batav-wolf-canis-batavorum.md); symbolic parallel — silent dominance without spectacle). **Legacy scene citation (do not use):** [diplomacy-aethelgard.md](../../chronicles/outdated/space-wolves-alaric-relations/diplomacy-aethelgard/chapter.md). **Exemption:** the five **supreme posts** in the Tenth (Lord Castellan, Chief Apothecary, Master Chaplain, Master of the Forge, Chief Librarian) are **not** subject to the Watch prerequisite — ancient precedent of foundation and *Strategium* primacy. **All other** battle-brothers admitted to DECIMA (pure line, cohorts, organic support) **must** satisfy **(1)**. The same ***clausula retentionis antecedentis*** applies: brothers **already** on DECIMA’s roll at **051.M42** **retain** standing **without** retroactive Watch; **admissions** after the decree must certify **(1)**. Final approval remains **unanimous Triumvirate**; no appeal.

Above them all rests the Triumvirate — the Wall (Alaric), the Scalpel (Drusus), and the Judge (Varro) — the three minds that keep the beast chained to the forge.

## 4. DEPARTMENTAL ORDERS AND THE 5,000 CEILING

### 4.1 Counting principle

The Chapter’s **5,000** warriors are **every** Astartes with **gene-seed** and *Cohors Batavorum* status — **no** “extra” reserve off-books. **Line**, **specialty cohorts**, **Triumvirate**, **Master of the Forge**, **Chief Librarian**, and the full networks of Apothecaries, Techmarines, Chaplains, and campaign Librarians **count toward this ceiling**. There is no parallel legion of “invisible specialists”: anything not in stasis, exile, or capital sentence **consumes** one of five thousand slots.
