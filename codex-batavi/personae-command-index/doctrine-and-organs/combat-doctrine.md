# Combat Doctrine — *Cohors Batavorum*

**Status:** Wiki hub. **Index voice** is the Chapter combat doctrine (two-paragraph form). **Example battle plan** is a worked sequence, **not** law — the Chapter stays adaptable. Tables remain **pointers**.  
**Chapter tactics (category):** defensive doctrine · siege-hold · attrition.  
**Lean (one sentence):** The Batavian Iron-Guard prefer to **hold the wall** — attrition and siege-density under **Friction Geometry** — not lightning assault, terror-cult, or “better at everything.”

---

## Combat doctrine

The Batavian Iron-Guard do not hunt the war. They price the ground until the war must come, and they make that coming ruinously expensive. In a galaxy where Space Marines of every denomination are defined by surgical strike and ceaseless force, the Batavi receive the tide. Refineries, silos, and hive corridors become channels already measured; interlocking bolt-fire waits where the mathematics said the bodies would arrive. They fight in *Analytical Silence* — no hymn, no howl — *scuta* magnet-locked into a walking wall, power gladius finding the joint, shoulder and fist the second blade that finishes what the thrust began. Pure line is three densities of that same hold: Suppression for the flesh that floods the channel, Ruin for the hull that would break it, Siege for the meter that must not be given.

That hold is not one stripe’s vanity. Stasis is the wall that will not chase a taunt into empty air. Silence walks hab, dock, and tunnel as a sensor, hunting the face too perfect and the gait that does not belong, so the cage is not opened from within. Shadows close the dark already priced — ducts, rafters, the kill-box a steward thought was blind — knife and gladius, never the saw that would name them. Nullity keeps the hourglass upright, cobalt and haruspex together, so spell and veil cannot unmake the geometry the rest are dying to keep. One grammar, four scars, three rims: the enemy bleeds on static iron, and the victory that counts is the one no one cheers. They hold.

Employment of the four specialty fifties and the three line rims remains [`specialty-cohorts.md`](../../arsenal-and-logistics/specialty-cohorts.md) and [`military-hierarchy.md`](military-hierarchy.md) §3.4. The doctrine block does not script a battle order. The example below does — as illustration only.

## Example battle plan (typical mesh defense)

**Fence:** One priced hour on an industrial hive or forge district. Not a named campaign. Not *Adversarii at Portas*. Not a script every Legatus must run. Drop, board, and assault remain theater tools. A **Vexilla** already carries all four specialty fifties and the three line rims inside the same five companies — nothing is “summoned later” from another Chapter. Numbers below are reader order. Once contact is live, **cage, grind, close, and hourglass run together**.

**Before the seal.** Officers argue the plan as *Remonstratio*: break, place, proposal. After the *Alabarda* is planted, there is no debate. Execution is *Analytical Silence*. Mortal PDF and Guard may already share the same streets under **Shared Sweat** — same line of fire, plans offered alongside their commanders, chain of command unbroken. They are not Batavi numbers and not a second army the Chapter pretends to own.

**Ivory prices.** Silence walks hab, dock, ration market, and service tunnel — full plate or discreet *aquila* — as the sensor package. They do not hunt a single trick. They hunt **pattern**: gait, breath, gaze, the soap that does not belong, the worker who is too perfect. Where the enemy mimics the Imperium, ivory is first triage. They select mouths and cells. They do not replace *Lex Imperialis*. Tickets go to black pauldrons and to the Legatus relay.

**The cage is drawn.** Stasis and Siege-rim lock *scuta* into a walking wall. Sacrificial barriers force the tide into streets already measured. Gunmetal will not chase a taunt into empty air; they fight dirty and still, and they do not break formation. The Romulus line is marked. One meter beyond it is not a withdrawal problem. It is a retake-or-die problem. Redundancy is already written: lose a sector, and the next firing position is stronger.

**The tide is received.** Suppression fills the channels with interlocking bolt-fire. Ruin takes the hulls that would break the cage. There is no hymn and no howl. Where the press reaches the shields, the power gladius finds the joint and the second blade — shoulder, fist, elbow — finishes the kill as logistics, not a duel. The ideal end-state is already in motion: the enemy bleeding out against static iron before they ever own the first contact line.

**Black closes.** Shadows do not price. They execute Silence-signed targets — or a Legatus emergency tick. Ducts, rafters, chem pits, the junction a steward thought was blind. Combat knife and power gladius. No chainsword; the saw would name them. Smoke, hatch-charges, vertical closure. Ivory still owns which mouth dies. Black owns the dark it dies in.

**Cobalt holds the hourglass.** Haruspices feed hazard class; they do not wear the stripe. Nullity stabilizes what the veil tries to unmake — illusion, witch-mark, the spell that would open a channel the mathematics did not price. Cobalt shields minds, occludes hull and trench, and instructs the kill. If the enemy is only flesh and steel, cobalt still stands on the lattice: insurance, not ornament. If the enemy is also Warp, this is the hour the rest of the wall stays real.

**The meter.** When the grind is honest, no one cheers. Gene-seed is recovered if a brother falls. The cage is left standing. The tithe streets still work. They hold.

## What this page is

Homebrew Chapter articles file a **Combat Doctrine**: how the Chapter *prefers* to fight (one or two specializations), not a list of every Adeptus Astartes capability. The *Codex Astartes* teaches a balanced combined-arms approach; every Chapter can still fight every mode. Doctrine is **emphasis**.

This hub names that emphasis for the Batavi and points at the files that already hold the law.

**Not this page:** tabletop army rules named “Combat Doctrines” (Devastator → Tactical → Assault cycles). Do not import that game cycle as Batavi field law.
