## II. College (no single sovereign)

**Custos Ascensum** is the **sitting of nine**. The nine **Procuratores Ascensum** (PRIMA–NONA) **are** the college. There is **no** PRIMA-lieutenant Custos and **no** Corbec / Legatus PRIMA ownership of ascent.

**Speaker:** The nine **elect** one of their number. The speakership **rotates**. Any Vexilla may hold it. The Speaker is the barracks and parchment **face** — he does **not** become the office.

**Speaker keeps his home hat.** Election does **not** vacate **Procurator Ascensum — [SEAT]**. He remains that seat’s local overseer while he speaks for the college.

**Named Speaker this rotation:** **unnamed** (doctrine only).

**DECIMA:** **No** Procurator, **no** college seat, **no** circuit stop. The Tenth takes **finished** brothers ([military-hierarchy.md](military-hierarchy.md) §3).

## IV. Procurator Ascensum (per Vexilla)

Each **Vexilla PRIMA–NONA** maintains one **Procurator Ascensum** — local overseer of:

* neophyte / scout-equivalent cells attached to that **Vexilla** on campaign;  
* forge-labor rotation when not deployed;  
* that seat’s **voice and vote** inside **Custos Ascensum**;  
* host duty for circuit attachments (§V).

**Typical rank:** lieutenant or senior sergeant **instructor** billet — not a **Legatus** duty.

**Filed title:** **Procurator Ascensum — [SEAT]** (e.g. *Procurator Ascensum — QVINTA*).

**DECIMA:** **No** *Procurator Ascensum*.

## V. Circuit (neophyte school / young-brother stamp)

**Order lock:** The eight seats **PRIMA excepted** have **no** fixed study order. **PRIMA is always last.** That is when the neophyte learns Noviomagus as **template** — pedagogy, not sovereignty.

| Who | What | Ceiling |
|-----|------|---------|
| **Neophyte** | **Full** study attachment at each of the eight, any order, then **PRIMA last** | **Outside** the 5,000 until sealed |
| **Young sealed brother** | **Short** stamp-tour of each of the nine (same rule: eight any order, **PRIMA last**) — graduated on each seat, not schooled again | Stays on **home 500**; Home Legatus still owns him |

**DECIMA** is not a stop.

**Custos** files who moves and when. Host **Procurator** receives, teaches, stamps, and sends on. **Genetor Vexilli** may veto clearance; **Confessor Vexilli** holds conduct; **Wolfkeeper liaison** attaches per cycle.

## VII. Mesh recruitment (what the college coordinates)

**Pools:** Nine Phalanx mortal sources — Noviomagus underhive and forge cadres, Aethelgard pressure zones, Incus-Gravis high-G settlements, Khan-Bator granary stacks, Threshold reef cultures, etc. ([chapter-organization.md](../chapter-organization.md) § Recruitment).

**Custos files:**

* trial calendars across seats (Aethelgard Spire leap, Incus gravity academy, Noviomagus gearing rite, etc.);  
* **circuit** routing (§V);  
* aspirant quota vs **5,000** ceiling math (neophytes **outside** combat grid until elevation);  
* failed-aspirant disposition (serf track, industrial labor — not heralded);  
* neophyte scout / heavy-labor attachment routing among the nine Procurators;  
* proximity-substrata witness increment handoff to Judex filtration when habit crosses *vox* law ([proximity-substrata-and-witness-inheritance.md](proximity-substrata-and-witness-inheritance.md)).

**Custos does not:** perform implant surgery, rewrite penance tables, or replace **Genetor** clearance.
