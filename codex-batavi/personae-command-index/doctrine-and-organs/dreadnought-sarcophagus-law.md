## 1. Not a marine in a mech

An interned brother is a **Dreadnought**. The page must show the **pros and cons** of the coffin, not a living Astartes wearing a larger body.

**Pros:** walking fortress, heavy mount, terror, a mind the Chapter refused to lose.  
**Cons:** no face, no table, no kitchen, no kneel-with-the-neophyte; *auspex* instead of eyes; pain of a body that is not there; years in the tank arrive already decided; if the hull dies he does not climb out. He is not assigned to a Vexilla as a line walking asset.

**Hard exclude:** Contemptor gait as “he still runs like Radulf.” Leviathan as siege-god first. Redemptor as a new hull teaching the **before**. *Castra-Lupus* remains living hypertrophied plate — Alaric is **not** entombed.

Interned brothers **count** inside their Vexilla’s **500**. The hull does not make a 501st.

## 3. Shared wake clock (all coffins)

Witness-wake and war-wake spend the **same** budget. Hours in teaching are hours not in the tank. If a coffin is a daily professor he is a stale guard dog. Mentor hours are **scheduled and rare**.

| Mode | What it is | Duration |
|------|------------|----------|
| **Stasis** (default) | Amniotic sleep in the assigned vault. | Centuries-scale between wars. |
| **Witness-wake** | Teaching, merit salute, archive. Usually vault-adjacent, cold. | **Hours**, not campaign days. |
| **War-wake** | Full arming, sealed release. | **Days** to a **couple of weeks**, then mandatory tank. |

**Furor in the coffin (quality first, a little duration second):** the interned brother stays **present** — less vox-loop, less century-slip — while he is up. He also gets a **small** extra slice before the tank is mandatory. **Not** months as default. **Not** a combat buff. Furor heat remains a tax (Drusus sedation, Varro soul-wear). This is **not** *Medicinae* remission of Furor; warp transit and calendar skew do **not** “heal” the Wolf’s Curse.

**Overdraw** (keeping him up past the cap) **lengthens the next sleep** and can scrape cognition. *Reclusiam* files soul-wear as a real limit.

Three-office split: **Drusus** vitals, **Varro** soul, **Kadmos** machine.

## 4. Geography and spend

**Bifrons-Ferrum:** Chapter arsenal tomb. **Spendable** Dreadnoughts sleep here. Legatus / Kadmos warrants may walk them to ordinary and dire wars **before** Portas. SEXTA may hold a vault war **without** touching the Old Wolf.

**Noviomagus:** **Radulf** alone in a **DECIMA / Castellan** cradle. He is the dog of the **Castra Vetera** polity because he never left the heart. Moving that coffin to a threatened gate is itself a strategic event. The Hall of Blades on Bifrons may stay the plaque-oath ([`technical-datasheet.md`](technical-datasheet.md)); it does not require him as a touring professor.

## 5. Radulf Castrin — *The Old Wolf* (DECIMA)

**Chassis:** **Castraferrum**. Loadout **TBD**.

**First job:** Chapter **mentor / Dreadnought ancient** (same English word as Vexilla **Ancient**, **different office**). He teaches the story of the Batavi, **especially before they were Batavi** — what the Chapter lost, what it gained, what it means to be Batavi. Reclamation is the birth ([`the-reclamation.md`](../events-and-chronologies/the-reclamation.md)); pre-Reclamation service remains **TBD** and is his charter. **DECIMA** has **no** Vexilla Ancient; the **justice vexillum** stays on the **Emperor’s Wolf**.

**Second job:** guard dog of the polity. **War-wake only** on ***Adversarii at Portas***. He is the **only** Dreadnought deployed **on that protocol**; every other coffin was already spent. **Order:** **Alaric only**. Not Graile, not Gervas, not a Legatus.

**Furor at interment (confirmed):** lucidity plus a little extra wake, as §3 — **not** a cheat code.
