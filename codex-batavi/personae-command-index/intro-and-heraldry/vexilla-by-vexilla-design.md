# Vexilla by Vexilla — design register

**Status:** **Locked** — default Chapter face on every **PRIMA–NONA** cloth; **LATIN-CAPS** ordinal; **Ancient** bearer (one per Vexilla); **PRIMA** obligation exception; **DECIMA** justice charge. Unique per-seat field-3/4 art is **dropped**.  
**Law:** [chapter-identity.md](chapter-identity.md) (wolf, gladius, waves, **no moon**); [visual-identity-paint-guide.md](../../visual-identity-paint-guide.md); banner stencil [`vexillum-template-lineart-castellum.png`](../../lore-images/vexillum-template-lineart-castellum.png). Legacy yard-on-haft art [`alabarda-vexillum-layout-reference.png`](../../lore-images/alabarda-vexillum-layout-reference.png) is **obsolete**.

## 1. Standard panel — default Astartes face (SECVNDA–NONA)

Every **Vexilla banner** (**SECVNDA–NONA**) is a **Codex-style company standard at Vexilla scale** (one cloth for the **500**, not for each captain’s **100**). **No** unique theater icon. **No** unique victory charge. **No** company banners under this cloth.

**Bearer:** **Ancient** (vanilla English) — one per Vexilla, **Legatus retinue**. The **1st company captain** does **not** hold the pole. The Legatus *Alabarda* does **not** mount this cloth. **No** company Ancients.

| Charge | Content |
|--------|--------|
| **Chapter icon** | Canonical achievement: crimson roundel logic, rectilinear **white wolf**, **orange** lens triangles (**105°**), **white** horizontal **gladius** + black contour, **waves** (full or abbreviated). Same identity as [`chapter-seal-official.png`](../../lore-images/chapter-seal-official.png). May **reduce** detail for scale, not **replace** charges. |
| **Strategium ordinal** | **LATIN-CAPS** seat name (**SECVNDA**–**NONA**) — high contrast. Must read at distance and when half-furled. Brothers speak this name alone (e.g. **QVINTA**), not “fifth Vexilla.” |

**Honours:** *Strategium* may add **restrained** Codex-style honour marks the Castellan allows (campaign tallies, not a second Chapter mascot and not a unique seat “greatest success” panel). Seat operations stay in the **chronicle / AAR register** (§3) — they are **not** unique banner art.

**Furled:** Chapter icon + ordinal must remain visible on the outer roll. Furl, unfurl, and ground-pin are **Ancient** authority under the Legatus’s geometry.

## 2. PRIMA (same default face, **obligation** exception)

PRIMA uses the **same Chapter icon + LATIN-CAPS ordinal**. It does **not** fly a unique forge mascot or a triumph panel. The exception is **permanent remembrance** the First must **uphold and rectify**.

| Charge | Content |
|--------|--------|
| **Chapter icon** | As above — PRIMA is still *Cohors Batavorum*. |
| **Ordinal** | **PRIMA** — LATIN-CAPS seat name. |
| **Obligation** | **Remembrance geometry** on border or a reserved canton — *not* the traitor’s glory. Suggested **visual grammar** (pick one or combine sparingly): **broken cog** (see §2.1); **expunged blank** where a name was struck; **ash horizon** stripe (**Malakor V**); **broken chain** link count **500** (lost brothers); **no** readable *Lupercal* titling. **Doctrine framing:** §2.2. |

### 2.1 PRIMA border — broken cog

**Primary treatment:** **Cogwheel border** around the panel with **one tooth missing** or **the rim split** — **half-broken cog** reads as **industrial duty interrupted** by treason and **re-forged** under the Noviomagus re-seat.

**Fallback:** If the painter cannot land “broken half” at small scale, use **intact cog border** with a **single gap** (one tooth **not drawn** / **void**) at **six o’clock** — same meaning, simpler silhouette.

### 2.2 PRIMA obligation — why shame on the banner does not break the line

**Design rule (legibility):** The remembrance stays **symbolic** — cog fracture, void plate, ash band, chain tally — so **outsiders** default to “industrial PRIMA mark”; **insiders** supply **debt**. *Strategium* forbids **slogan text** that names the traitor or *Lupercal* on the cloth; the wound is **geometry**, not a taunt enemies can quote across a kill-zone.

**Induction line (mandatory brief, PRIMA transfers and neophyte orientation):**  
> *“**Debt we carry — not a crime you committed.** The lattice failed once; your file starts under that weight so it does not happen again.”*

*Reclusiam* may phrase it as liturgy; *Strategium* logs it as **aptitude receipt** — same sentence, no elaboration in formation (fits *Analytical Silence*).

**Formal register name (minutes, *Armourium* annex):** **Rectification charge** or **obligation field** — *not* “shame panel” in tithe-facing scrolls — to avoid Administratum misread as **penal** company.

**Friction (accepted):** Some battle-brothers **resent** flying another generation’s fracture; *Medicinae* and captains treat chronic **bitterness toward the vexillum** as **watch item**, not heresy — discipline only if it **breaks** fire geometry or order. That resentment is **usable fiction**: the standard is heavy **on purpose**, not because the Chapter mistakes humiliation for proof.

**Benefit (why keep it):** The obligation mark is **instrument** — **no denial** under smoke, **bounded guilt** (only PRIMA wears PRIMA’s scar), **forward** duty: *rectify*, not *atrophy*.

***Strategium* memorandum (obligation precedent, not a triumph AAR):** [memorandum-prima-rectification-precedent.md](../../chronicles/vexillum-register/memorandum-prima-rectification-precedent.md).

## 4. DECIMA (anchor guard): **justice vexillum**

**Not** a Legatus *Alabarda* clone. **Not** a Vexilla **Ancient**. DECIMA flies a **separate battle standard** whose **dominant charge** is:

> **A feral wolf — transhuman lupine monstrosity, not the clean chapter stencil — transfixed or slain by a sword** (gladius-type, **white** blade / **black** contour to echo the seal’s blade law).

**Meaning (register language):** execution of **First Legatus treason** — the **death of Valerius’s path** (*Lupercal* claim, PRIMA rot) by **Triumvirate will** and the Castellan’s blade at **Malakor V**. It is **not** a trophy skin; it is **doctrine made cloth** — *Varro*’s oath and the Cull geometry in heraldic form.

**Companion fields (still four, but hierarchy flips):**

| Priority | Field | Content |
|----------|--------|--------|
| **Dominant** | **Justice charge** | Feral wolf **killed by sword** (as above). |
| **Secondary** | **Chapter icon** | Reduced seal or **wolf head only** — must not compete with the justice scene; often **chief** strip or **fly** quarter. |
| **Tertiary** | **Ordinal** | **DECIMA** — LATIN-CAPS; can be **stamped** on haft or **small canton** so the central image stays the wolf and blade. |
| **Quaternary** | **Anchor** | **Triumvirate / anchor guard** — minimal **three-dot** or **triangle** (Wall–Scalpel–Judge) **or** plain **grey band** (*instrumentum*). |

**Bearer:** **LVPVS IMPERATORIS** (**Emperor’s Wolf**) — primary field bearer; incumbent **Markus Graile**. Early commission logs name a redacted **First Ancient** before the office was stamped **LVPVS IMPERATORIS** — that is **DECIMA cloth history**, not the **PRIMA–NONA Ancient** office. *Not* interchangeable with Legatus *Alabarda* logistics; relay rules may **differ** (Triumvirate net).

***Armourium* / *Reclusiam* register annex (justice charge precedent):** [register-decima-malakor-justice.md](../../chronicles/vexillum-register/register-decima-malakor-justice.md). **Narrative (051.M42 commission):** [decima-justice-vexillum.md](../../chronicles/silent-vigil/valerian-heresy/decima-justice-vexillum/chapter.md). **Sentence:** [malakor-v/chapter.md](../../chronicles/silent-vigil/valerian-heresy/malakor-v/chapter.md).
