## II. Origin register — Vigilax (110.M42)

On **Vigilax Prime**, Malevolent forces conducted an **unauthorized** purge under territory the Batavi held by mandate. **Varro** applied Castra Vetera law in **Serragris**; **Legatus III** / **Drusus** triangulated **Marna-Down** before escalation completed. Alaric killed the **Malevolent Chapter Master** bare-handed at the reckoning audience after an insult referencing **Valerius** execution.

**Lasting state:** undeclared hatred; Malevolent withdrawal as open wound — not orderly victory courtesy.
