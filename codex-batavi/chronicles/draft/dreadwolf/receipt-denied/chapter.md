# Receipt denied

**Status:** **Draft scratch** — refinement prose; **not** on the Dreadwolf read chain. Does **not** supersede published [*terminus*](../../../dreadwolf/terminus/chapter.md).  
**When:** **168.M42** — Luna Sigma, Noviomagus system (Battle of Sigma Moon), outer storage spine.  
**Unit:** **The Lost** (*Amissi*) — **Lieutenant Einar Dreadwolf**; **Varro** (Judex); **Legatus Armin Sturmwahl** (QVINTA attached, not chain-of-command).  
**Opponent (scratch only):** **Khârn** — mythic upright champion. Published register remains unnamed until rewrite ordered.  
**Beat lock:** [`../../../dreadwolf/terminus/SCAFFOLD.md`](../../../dreadwolf/terminus/SCAFFOLD.md)  
**Kit (scratch):** QVINTA **chain-claws** — three wrist chainblades over clawed Exanimus hand (chain-first culture); not published Wolf Talons lock until kit retcon ordered.

Luna Sigma had never learned to be a moon. It had learned to be a ledger — ore crushers, convict bunks, servitor rails, ferrocrete that remembered every invoice stamped into it. When Otho’s *Apex* warning sealed the Warp channels, the page waited for ink that would not come from Administratum quills.

**Armin Sturmwahl** brought **QVINTA** onto the First Vexilla’s outer ring to cut World Eaters geometry before mass learned how to breathe. Forty meters back, **Varro** stood where rear command could still see the spine without pretending to see the Warp.

The Judex had already made the ugly choice.

The moon was worse than the leash. Angron’s shadow was thicker than any *Amissi* tolerance chart. So Varro did not call Einar back when the gauge climbed. He filed the suspension in the part of his mind that would hate him later: **dire necessity**. Wild Hunt permitted. Reason offline.

**Einar Dreadwolf** took the storage spine like a debt collector who had finally been told the correct address.

Assault Intercessor **Mk X Tacticus** in *Amissi* paint — black pauldrons, Chapter wolf and chained wolf-skull **X**, Mephiston Red only on gauntlets and boot feet, half-helm locking nose and jaw while mane and **Nails** ran free. Jump pack for speed, not glory. On both wrists: **chain-claws** — three long chainblade bars mounted dorsal over clawed Exanimus hands, QVINTA saw-law welded into *Amissi* watch, motors screaming, teeth hungry for traitor meat.

He did not fight like a Batavi under *Axiom of Reason*.

He fought like a man who had been waiting ninety-four years to be paid.

The World Eaters arrived as pressure — chainaxe rhythm, boltless charges, Massacre written into skull iron. Batavi line answered with short bursts and gladius finishes. Invoice.

Einar answered with **Wild Hunt**.

He hit the first cluster as Needles vector made flesh: jump-burn, traction claws biting ruptured deck, three bars on each fist chewing ceramite into wet ribbon. A champion’s head came half-loose. Brass hunger woke in the air like a receipt printer.

Einar crushed the vault under a boot until bone became grit unfit for any throne.

— Purge the traitor — he said, voice tide-flat under Nails scream. — Starve the Throne.

Then he stopped explaining, because explanation was a luxury rage did not refund.

Eightbound came through a ruptured bulkhead in red steam and bind-stink — possessed mass, lacerators, the kind of thing that ended ordinary lieutenants. Einar met them with saw-mass and hatred older than their bindings. One lost an arm at the elbow. One lost the face. One tried to take his skull for Brass and received a dorsal rip that opened the chest like a ration tin. Exalted Eightbound followed — heavier, louder, more certain of kinship with anything that wore Nails.

Einar showed them kinship was a lie.

He tore. He sliced. He spent World Eaters lives as **coin** — payment for Moraine-Nine, for eleven brothers gifted death before an enemy could bite their souls, for every second the Red Demon had tried to rename him. Traitor blood painted the spine. Skulls that might have risen he smashed, burned through, left as slag. No bowl. No lift. No honest offering.

Lesser daemons bled into the corridor as the veil thinned — pinkish brass things, half-formed slaughter-shapes. Some of them died fast. Some of them died surprised. A lesser–Greater presence pushed through a warp-sore in the deck plates and found, for the first time in its short ugly life, that a Batavi Lost brother could force it back a step, then two, then a wet retreat into the sore with chain-teeth still chewing its heel.

Varro’s gauge went past every tolerance filed since Moraine-Nine.

Armin cut World Eaters off the western spur and felt the Wild Hunt as weather — wrong, necessary, unpaid.

Somewhere behind Einar’s teeth, the Red Demon laughed without a mouth.

Somewhere farther than mouths, something vast and brass-colored took note of an unpaid column becoming a riot.

He came upright through his own side’s spray — too clean for the horde’s stumbling liturgy, as if the brass counted only what he collected. Gorechild’s teeth were already wet. The helm that wore amusement like a second face did not bother with a challenge-horn.

**Khârn.**

Einar did not need a slate. The Nails knew the frequency. The Red Demon surged toward it the way a tide surges toward a moon it has already claimed.

Einar held nothing that still answered to *Axiom*. He held debt.

— For the Skull Throne — Khârn said. — For the Blood God. Give me your head, little wolf. You have been denying my master his due.

Around them the spine still died in side corridors. The duel did not ask permission. It made its own kill-bowl out of ferrocrete and heat.

Einar’s answer was one sentence, QVINTA law bent through rage:

— I deny the skulls to the Red Tyrant.

Khârn laughed as if the denial itself were an offering.

Gorechild came in — butcher’s economy, not waste. Einar met it with chain-claws, three bars screaming, wrist motors howling. Sparks. Teeth on teeth. Impact through ceramite into bone. Both bodies received the same lie: *you can end this now.*

Neither ended it.

They traded at near-peer violence. Jump-pack cut. Traction bite. Axe taking a pauldron lip. Chain-claws opening Khârn’s thigh plate and making the Betrayer limp three steps that should have been fatal and were not — favour keeping the invoice open. Einar took a rib-crack that should have dropped a line brother and stayed standing because hatred had already spent the drop.

Skill for skill. Will for will.

The difference was **boon**.

Khorne prolonged the fight and deepened the bleed so the unpaid column could not close early. Khârn lasted past mortal accounting because the Throne liked him. Einar lasted past mortal accounting because the Throne wanted interest — and because Wild Hunt does not stop when stopping would be wise.

Gorechild found the chain-claw housings at last.

Metal screamed. Motors burst. Three bars on the right fist tore free in a spray of teeth and cable. The left followed a heartbeat later — dorsal mounts ripped, saws dead, QVINTA chain-first law reduced to wreckage on the deck.

Einar did not stop.

He went into Khârn with **Exanimus** alone — clawed fingers, jaw, mass, Nails song without industrial translation. Fists like Needles storm. Talons that were flesh and curse. He ripped a World Eater’s discarded plate aside and seized two **chainaxes** from the dead, one in each hand, and the sound that came out of him was not a motto. It was a century of held Furor finally spent without permission.

Hair thickened along the mane. Muscle packed wrong under Tacticus shell. Shoulders climbed. The marine visage frayed. Time stopped pretending to be fair.

Khârn kept pace — smiling inside the helm — because Brass kept pumping him while Einar climbed toward a shape the Chapter named only in sealed annexes.

Then the chain snapped.

**Versibar.**

Not brink. Crossing. Mass rewriting. Eyes yellow and slit and suddenly, terribly, **clear** in a way that was not human clarity. The Red Demon — the second beast bound inside the first since *Kzorn’s Maw* — met something it could not ride.

Warp immunity took the socket away.

In the sealed sight Varro alone carried, the soul geometry changed in one merciless image: no longer the man holding wolf and demon in a tripartite bind. Only the **wolf free** — and the wolf **killing the demon**. Freedom from Khorne bought at the price of everything else. The Lost brother spent as war-form. The person unpaid.

On the field, Khorne’s favour had nowhere left to plug into Einar. It poured into Khârn instead. Gorechild came brighter. The Betrayer’s wounds closed wrong. The duel continued as asymmetric cruelty: curse-biology versus Brass-pumped champion, wolf free of influence and free of the man who had carried it.

They broke ferrocrete. They broke each other. Chainaxes chewed. Axe answered. Einar’s war-form surge should have ended any ordinary butcher. Khârn was not ordinary. The Throne would not let the Versibar receipt go cheap.

Khârn pinned him against a ruptured containment wall — mass to mass, Gorechild rising for the kind of kill that ends in lifted bone and spilled offering, the perfect double column: skull **and** war-form.

For one thin interval — not peace, not reason restored, only **duty muscle memory** firing through the free wolf — the marine visage returned far enough to find a thumb.

The sealed port at the atlas joint. ***Spina Cineris.*** Drusus’s trim. Varro’s watch. Ninety-four years of the last denial.

Einar did not give Khârn the head.

He did not leave the Throne a Versibar trophy.

He paid the only coin Brass could not reclassify as worship.

A muffled detonation — not wall-breach, not bolt — **inside** the collar seal.

Molecular destabilization unmade the cranial vault without granting the galaxy a bowl to raise. Brain and occipital lattice flashed to ferrous ash and ionized vapor. Cervical flash welded the neck seal before spray could become liturgy.

Khârn recoiled from a body that collapsed while the half-helm still held — war-form mass already failing into ash-accounted ruin.

No skull lifted clean. No jet painted the ferrocrete for Khorne’s accountants. Only grit, slag smell, and a faceplate fogged with dust, not rain.

Both columns unpaid. Demon dead in the soul. Wolf free only by becoming extinction.

Armin reached the wall too late to strike, too early to pretend ignorance. He saw the ash vent, the port sealed, the brother already gone in every sense that mattered to Brass.

Varro crossed the distance without hurry.

He looked at Khârn once — empty hands that had expected trophy weight — then at the collapsed armor. Behind the Executor wolf-skull he filed the sealed image he would never speak on open rolls: wolf free, demon killed, man spent.

— No skull — Varro said, voice flat as a filed edge. — No spill. Hold denied you.

Khârn screamed and came for the Judex. Varro’s *Crozius* answered in Sentence mode; the geometry of that exchange belongs to the forty-two Batavi the moon counted among its dead, not to this scratch.

Angron would descend minutes later. Alaric would walk to meet him. QVINTA would be pulled into evacuation vectors when Furor brothers tried to advance on the Castellan’s duel.

**Einar Dreadwolf** would not be on the stretcher list.

Drusus’s teams would find *Amissi* armor at the storage spine — Mk X Tacticus, *Spina Cineris* pin spent, progenoid tissue annihilated, harvest null. *Vermilion* filing: destroyed by terminis protocol, not by corruption verdict. What the body had become in the last minutes would be argued in sealed annexes for years. The ash did not care.

On the gunboat bay, with Luna Sigma shrinking on the auspex, Armin would not speak the QVINTA cadence aloud. He would touch fist to chest once — the same salute Einar had given when the cull blade rose at Moraine-Nine — and turn to the next vector.

Varro would close the line:

*In watch. Under the Deathwolf’s jaws. No soul-name. No precedent. No cult.*  
**Terminus 168.M42 — Luna Sigma. Receipt denied.**
