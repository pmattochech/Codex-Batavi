2. **Gene-seed / lineage** — Less important than chapter history/story for “vibe”; can subvert legion stereotypes (Ultramarines-line who ignore Codex; Salamanders-line who act like Space Wolves); Black Rage etc. are exceptions; iron hands chivalrous / Dark Angels tank-focused — all allowed.
3. **Cheat code: BE VAGUE** — Imperium record-keeping is terrible; mixed/abominational gene-seed survivable if uncertain; traitor seed loyalists hide shame; remembrancers can’t prove lineage — use rumors/denials.
4. **Embrace tropes** — 40K is satire of tropes; knightly, militaristic, etc. work; nuance from subverting trope (e.g. artillery chapter that doesn’t care about human casualties → Marines Malevolent-adjacent); camaraderie, accents, naming conventions follow naturally.
5. **Goals & rivalries** — Drive culture and fighting style (vengeance chapter vs young Inquisition attack dogs; tanks vs flamers/chainswords vs sword-school rivalry).
6. **Characters / leaders** — Council or unnamed Chapter Master possible but single named Chapter Master encouraged for fun; power creep bucket: Chapter Master + First Captain + Chapter Champion; split tropes across three; careful with named canon antagonists — don’t diminish them (wounded/humiliated beats “fought Kharn to standstill”); vagueness for implied feats (Chapter Master and Kharn both missing, returns “failed to slay”); same vagueness for character age (Dante ~1500–1600 oldest loyalist; marines die; Dreadnoughts excepted).
7. **Foundings (~30 total)** — Young all-Primaris → Ultimate Founding (27th/28th, Guilliman revival); mutations/near-renegade → 21st Cursed Founding; ancient chapter → vague “between 3rd and 6th, records lost”; **discourage Second Founding** (Black Templars, Crimson Fists, Flesh Tearers, Nova Marines, Mortifactors, Silver Skulls, Raptors, Grey Knights set impossibly high bar).
8. **Chapter size** — ~1000 marines Codex mark; slightly over/under OK; over 1000 causes Imperial problems (Astral Claws / Badab War); Black Templars thousands = rare exception.
9. **Allies** — Should emerge naturally from goals/tropes; cross-homebrew narrative fun; guidelines help mesh with canon.
10. **“In a nutshell” test** — God Emperor’s New Groove (Instagram): e.g. “apathetic Salamanders” or “loyalist Night Lords” — one-line premise then expand.
