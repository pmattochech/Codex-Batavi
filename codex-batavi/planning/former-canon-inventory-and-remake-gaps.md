## VI. Success criteria (“same amount of material”)

Treat remake as “caught up” when roughly:

1. **Wiki rooms** exist again for: identity, org, doctrine, curse-ops, atlas, bestiary (enemy + curse), relations, arsenal, lexicon.  
2. **Named density** ≈ old People hub (apex + officers + formation leads + mortals + at least one custodian face).  
3. **Chronicle scaffolds** cover birth, wall-war, internal fracture, curse-ops era, and multi-face enemy serials — even if prose is unfinished.  
4. **Old serials** are either promoted under new locks, parked in `outdated/`, or explicitly deleted by user order — no silent half-canon.

Until then, former canon on disk remains the **density baseline**; remake notes remain the **spine**.
