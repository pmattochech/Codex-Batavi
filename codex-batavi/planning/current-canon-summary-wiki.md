**INSTRUMENTUM · SUBJECT HEADER** (Ordo joint interest · *Castra Vetera* filter asset · Magenta+)

**Designation (Imperial):** Chapter *Cohors Batavorum* — vernacular **Batavian Iron-Guard**. **Civic / local gloss:** *Der Batav*. **Theater:** Ultima · Imperium Nihilus · **Insula Tenebrarum** mesh · polity **Castra Vetera**. **Chapter world (forge-heart):** Noviomagus. **Open Founding / progenitor:** **UNKNOWN / REDACTED**. **Observed strength:** ~**5,000** battle-brothers in **ten Vexilla** (non-*Codex* ceiling — tolerated under super-tithe and filter necessity; see *Limes Lupus* collar filings). **Mottoes on record:** **Unbent** (Chapter); battle answer **We hold.**; formal faith line *The Emperor dictates, we comply.* **Assessed role:** Permanent **wall / filter** against inward tide (xenos, Chaos traffic, renegade void, plague-routes). Not a crusade Chapter by preference.

**Auditor note:** Subject is widely disliked or held in suspicion across ordinary Imperial commands. Local mesh populations and forces that live behind their line more often tolerate or rely on them. Utility and tithe performance remain the practical reason Terra has not discarded the asset.

**I. Identity as filed.** The *Cohors Batavorum* present on every open Administratum and tithe scroll as a Space Marine Chapter of **undisclosed Founding** and **undisclosed progenitor**. No sealed receipt shown to this cell confirms a parent Legion, a numbered Founding, or a single gene-template house. Observers repeatedly note **wolf-marked** or **legion-echo** phenotype and **stabilised chimeric** expression under high clearance. The Chapter’s own public posture is to **accept the mystery** and refuse genealogy as morale or law. In their mouth, the **only ancestor that counts is the Emperor**.

Heraldry (white frontal wolf, horizontal gladius, associated charges) is treated by remembrancers as **art history and recognition mark**, not as a gene-seed certificate. When wolf-marked outsiders (notably sons of Fenris) press resemblance as proof of Russ-line descent, Batavi answers on record are flat: insistence on plate-reading will invent every old Legion; formula does not matter; open filing stays **unknown**.

They are not, on any open layer this cell will endorse, a Traitor Legion successor Chapter. Chimeric architecture is medically discussed in sealed *Apothecarion* channels; a complete donor manifest is **not** available to ordinary Ordos desks.

**II. Name and myth.** Imperial paper did not invent the name that sticks. On **Aethelgard**, during the birth-war later filed as **the Reclamation**, coastal witnesses named the grey giants emerging from surf and swamp with xenos blood still wet: ***Der Batav*** — after the local apex predator *Canis Batavorum*, the tide-wolf that does not flee the Great Tide but uses it as hunting ground. **Cohors Batavorum** follows from the same weather of lungs and fear. The Chapter did not first mint itself in a council chamber; the people of the shore **verdicted** what they saw.

Auditor gloss: the subject’s self-image is therefore **island-born** and **witness-bound** — identity gold at Castra Vetera, not merely a fortress seized for ore. They style themselves neither fully of Terra’s heart nor of the open void: a covenant people whose job is the wall.

**III. Faith and mentality.** The Chapter does **not** present as Ministorum God-Emperor worshippers. Filed practice honors the **divinity the Emperor carries** — oath, Throne-weight, **Ancestor-Sovereign** — without chapel ecstasy. Liturgical contact with Ecclesiarchy on the mesh is friction-prone; do not mistake Ancestor-Sovereign language for compliant Ministorum catechism.

Operational philosophy on their own teaching: ***Axiom of Reason* (*Axioma Rationis*)** — emotion is a biological variable regulated by logic; service demands reason, not blind devotion; the beast in the blood is dominated for utility. ***Instrumentum Solum*** — the Astartes answers praise as **only a tool**. **Unbent** — remain upright under pressures that shatter other formations. Battle seal: **We hold.** When spoken by the Castellan under load, retreat is treated as geometric impossibility.

**Shared Sweat (*Aequalitas Machinae*):** off-line brothers labor in civilian forges, mines, and infrastructure beside the population; in war they staff plans **alongside** Imperial Guard command without bypassing chain of command. Filed effects: burns chimeric overload, anchors humanity against ego-detachment, imports local habit through fatigue rather than conversion theater.

**IV. Strategic purpose.** Subject’s preferred war is **not** the hunt across the stars. They **price the ground** until the war must come, then make that coming ruinously expensive. Combat lean: defence, siege-hold, attrition under **Friction Geometry** and *Analytical Silence* — interlocking fire, shield-wall density, power gladius and “second blade” close work. Specialty lattice (Silence / Shadows / Stasis / Nullity) and line rims keep that single grammar intact across the mesh.

They are the **wall against the tide** at Insula Tenebrarum: Chaos, xenos, renegade void, plague-traffic — **the tide is anything that would flood inward**. Passage doctrine: evil does not pass inward; need passes under witness, tithe, and geometry. Selective triage, not an open door.

**Size breach:** ~5,000 in ten Vexilla is **friction conveyor / replacement math** for endless mesh attrition and curse/biology spend — not vanity conquest. Super-tithe (≥15% over quota as floor, fat convoy ~once per 20-year anomaly window, large Chapter escort) makes the subsector economically hard to discard. Political collar (*Limes Lupus*) and Custodes oversight appear in later filings as the price of that illegality under Terra’s eye.

**V. Origin war (staff facts).** **the Reclamation** (*Recuperatio Vetera*), **pre-Cadia**. Forbidden alternate title: Foundation War. They become Batavi by taking Castra Vetera. Ingress as incomplete **Grey Hounds** / *Slate Cohort — Hound Pattern* (identity mystery; hull-born more than soil-born). Four campaign beats end in Ford/Ribbon **seal**. Villain architecture includes **Men of Iron** fence-work (late revelation, costly). When MoI are beaten, machine failure yields the **navel vortex** (stabilised enough not to swallow the subsector; still a wound).

Thereafter **the Hold** (~300 years, still pre-Cadia): nine seats, fence as cage most of the time, anomaly windows, always-overproduced tithe. **Valerian** brother-war during the Hold founds **DECIMA** and scars the specialty cohorts into being. Cicatrix converts cage to **V-shield**. Working present: ~310 years as Batavi (~300 Hold + ~12 Indomitus). Primaris intake is later **method** (Rubicon of eligible Firstborn, then all new marines Primaris made and raised in Castra) — **not** imported battle-brother tranches.

**VI. Assessed character (Ordos margin).** The subject reads to loyalist peers as cold, silent, socially constrained, and biologically unclean. They read themselves as tools on a wall. They will work beside mortals and Guard. They will not howl for propaganda. They will overpay the tithe. They will hold meters other Chapters would abandon for a cleaner victory elsewhere.

**Threat/utility balance (this desk):** High deviation (size, chimera, curse apparatus, faith divergence) offset by irreplaceable filter geography, industrial output, and demonstrated willingness to spend themselves on static iron. Recommend continued **joint overwatch**, not premature Excommunicate theater, unless sealed biology or political idolatry (see post-vigil internal cult incidents) escapes containment.

Inside the V, Warp remains normal; marsh channels still work; the island is **not** a dead zone. **Cicatrix** would swallow the micro-sector; it hits the reflector and **splits** — mesh sits in a **V** of still-realspace; cage becomes **V-shield**.

Cadence across the Hold: **able to open ~once per 20 years** — not monthly, not yearly tithe runs (~15 windows across ~300 Hold years). Hulls may jump to safe realspace beside the Rift (Nihilus or Sanctus) with tithe spoil; standing **comms** to Terra do not exist. Super-tithe always on (≥15% over quota floor); stockpile between windows; fat convoy when anomaly opens; large Chapter escort (post-DECIMA: DECIMA as core escort). **Badab lesson:** isolation is never an excuse to withhold.
