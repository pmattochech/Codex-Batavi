The Triumviratum is the supreme council that rules the Chapter, structured as a tripartite governing body where no single leader holds absolute domestic power. The council is composed of the highest representatives from each of the Chapter's core pillars:

- The Castellan (Directorio Militant): Serves as the high representative of the militant side and functions as the Chapter Master. While he lacks absolute rule domestically, he operates as the chief executive and the primary diplomatic president when dealing with the wider Imperium.

- The Chief Apothecary (Officio Liminalis): Serves as the high representative of physical survival, material maintenance, and genetic mutation containment.

- The High Chaplain (Synodus Animarum): Serves as the high representative of the spiritual authority, psychic defense, and psychological sanity tracking.

3.1 - Underneath this ruling council, the three operational offices execute their respective portfolios:

- The Directorio Militant is composed by, and their portfolio is:

  - The Castellan: Responsible for supreme strategic command, macro-warfare theater deployment, and acting as the diplomatic president of the Triumviratum.

  - The Master of the Hunt: Responsible for commanding the Chapter Champion tier, orchestrating stealth sabotage operations, and executing high-threat targets through isolated, elite specialist cells.

  - The Praetor-Purveyor: Responsible for the survival logistics of the attrition war, including the deployment of heavy vehicles, planetary drop-ship delivery networks, and the meticulous management of dwindling ammunition and fuel stockpiles.

- The Officio Liminalis is composed by, and their portfolio is:

  - The Apothecarion: Responsible for cellular restoration, blood-purification, and the preservation of the mutating Gene-Seed under the direction of the Chief Apothecary.

  - The Armament Forge: Responsible for the reclamation of battlefield scrap, weapon maintenance, and the integration of bionics into warped limbs.

  - The Censorium: Responsible for the strict monitoring of genetic drift, physical degradation tracking, and the containment or execution of the biologically corrupted.

- The Synodus Animarum is composed by, and their portfolio is:

  - The Reclusiam: Responsible for enforcing combat zeal, recording final testaments, and administering silent psychological absolution or memory-wipes to trauma-shattered brothers under the direction of the High Chaplain.

  - The Librarius: Responsible for warp navigation, psychic shielding, and chronicling the classified, uncensored history of the fringe-war.

  - The Observatorium Animi: Responsible for the active monitoring of cognitive drift, sleep deprivation, and behavioral deviations to permanently retire or execute brothers whose sanity has broken.

## Locked after Triumviratum offices (chat — not canon)

### War portfolio vs domestic council
- Inside the **Directorio Militant**, the **Castellan** has full command of the war section; the Directorio (Castellan + Master of the Hunt + Praetor-Purveyor) is **his war council**, not a second Triumviratum veto on every order.
- Domestic Chapter rule stays tripartite: Castellan is not absolute over Liminalis / Synodus by default.
- **What counts as war:** obvious war business → Castellan / Directorio. Grey cases → classified with **Triumviratum representatives**.
- **Liminalis / Synodus vs a war order:** they can and must stop a course that burns gene-stock or sanity past threshold — but they stay with the Directorio and **debate a better path that still hits the objective**. Not veto-and-walk; not militant loophole theater. True deadlock = Triumviratum crisis (later).

### Sponsor / inner Inquisition circle
- Ordos on the letterhead; High Lords countersign + direct overwatch (earlier lock). Mars binder, not owner.
- **Inner circle:** succession of **one Inquisitor-custodian** + tiny retinue (not a standing multi-seat cabal).
- Custodian **lies** (not only omits). Triumviratum may **hunt the truth in secret** when a lie is sensed **and** the hunt is strategically justified (enemy intel / wall survival) — not curiosity theater.
- **Map vs war:** full map (one wound, project under-structure, sponsor genealogy) stays with the custodian. **Reason for the war** (hold the ground, fight the host) is Castellan portfolio — run the war **without revealing** what must stay sealed.
- **Handoff:** a **sealed archive** exists; living brief to each new custodian is mostly **fragmentary**.
- Triumviratum / Chapter knowledge (earlier): enough / hunter-essentials — curse source, necessity, mission ahead; **not** in the inner circle; not the under-structure.

### Enemy / one wound (surface)
- One wound, two faces; link **not obvious** on the public brief.
- Public brief: hold the ground. Public enemy labels: **enemies of humanity** / cultists / xenos / heretics (messy bins).
- **Old “Church”** (Bloodborne-shape): once had protections/countermeasures; briefly **enslaved** the entity; mostly gone as an institution. Possible **Ecclesiarchy splinter** origin (why lightly: motor for renegade brothers who reject Ministorum-Imperium rot — detail not deep-dived).
- **Ascension remnant:** a handful who still want to ascend; **different agenda** from the free entity.
- **Hive continuation:** unregulated cult without Church safeguards → population mutates into **Skaven-energy beastmen** (port energy, not named Skaven IP). They **know** they feed the entity.
- **Under-empire:** starts in an underhive **away** from the Chapter base; caves until it is **everywhere** under the theater.
- **Entity:** awakened, once chained, now free; hates **all humanity**; fed by the vermin-cult ecology. **Name/nature parked** for later debate.
- Agenda collision (remnant vs entity-driven war) bites when someone tries to take **local sector command**.

### Renegades
- Paths: join ascension remnant (rarer), **lone**, or **small warbands** (last two most common).
- Chapter **condemns** desertion and treason; **fights** renegades when they make a presence or become a real problem — **not** a standing forever-hunt.
- **Not** a full Vexilla (e.g. scraped QVINTA) as forever renegade-hunters.
- **Master of the Hunt** elite cells execute the hunt when opened.
- **Synodus** places the **traitor brand**; **Directorio** owns mission, assessment, and execution.

### Explicitly deferred
- Abomination name/nature; deep renegade law; deposit identity (follows conflict model); full Vexilla scrape.
- Curse: clinical stage *detail* from old files (marks, Furor typology, Red Wolf, disciplines, *Amissi*, etc.) — keep or rewrite piece-by-piece in later passes. **Origin / ops / Versibar treatment** now opened below.

### How the Chapter works around it (working)
- Standing mass: brothers may be pre-Blood-Rite or Furor-stable; deeper stages are the attrition edge, not the clean roster’s whole story.
- **Synodus:** Chaplaincy as **anchor** (hold Furor / Exanimus from slipping early — “hot mind” brake) and **reaper** (absolution at Versibar kill).
- **Officio Liminalis:** flesh / gene / containment of the rite and mutation.
- **Directorio / Master of the Hunt:** when a Versibar must die in the field, **Hunt elite cells** (Champion-tier) execute — not a forever Vexilla purge seat.
- Mercy frame (theme, not song file): *remember not as a man, but as a vow* — kill the monster so the brother’s soul can be released to the Emperor; hunter must not freeze on the brother’s face.
- Three-way battlefield: vermin host + Chapter + Versibar hazard.
- Imperium hates them; theater that lives behind their wall tolerates them (already Chapter spine).

### Versibar treatment (locked direction)
- **Default:** hunt and slay (Hunt cells + Synodus absolution) — mercy / vow, not trophy.
- **Rarer cases:** kept **locked for study** (not every Versibar is immediately killed). Who holds the key (Liminalis / Triumviratum / custodian) — **TBD**.
